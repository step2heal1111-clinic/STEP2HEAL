Frequently Asked Questions

Get expert, clinical answers regarding physical rehabilitation, injury prevention, and pain management protocols.

A sports injury recovery timeline depends heavily on the severity (Grade I, II, or III) and tissue type (ligament, muscle, or tendon). Mild strains often heal within 2 to 4 weeks with structured rehabilitation exercises. Moderate-to-severe ligament tears (like ACL sprains) might require a comprehensive 6 to 9-month biomechanical rehabilitation protocol. Early physical therapy intervention dramatically reduces swelling and speeds up tissue recovery.

Chronic spinal pain is frequently caused by poor ergonomics and muscle imbalances. Posture correction therapy focuses on stretching overactive chest/neck muscles while strengthening deep cervical flexors and scapular stabilizers. Correcting spinal biomechanics reduces joint compression, preserves disc hydration, and optimizes nervous system signaling, leading to sustained, drug-free pain management.

During an online session (telehealth), our licensed clinical physical therapist performs a detailed biomechanical movement assessment via high-definition video. We analyze your joint alignment, range of motion, and pain triggers. Based on the evaluation, we design a customized rehab program, guide you live through corrective exercise techniques, and provide actionable ergonomic adjustments to accelerate home healing.

Dry needling causes minimal to no pain—most patients experience a brief "deep ache" or muscular twitch response when the needle reaches a trigger point. This localized stimulation causes immediate muscular relaxation, improves vascular flow, flushes out metabolic waste, and triggers a localized healing cascade, making it a highly effective modality for sports recovery and chronic myofascial pain management.
